# Operations Management

This section focuses on applying operational management concepts and analytical techniques to improve business efficiency, productivity and organizational performance.

## Key Focus Areas

- **Process Analysis and Optimization**
- **Operational KPI Analysis**
- **Productivity and Performance Measurement**
- **Workflow Management**
- **Inventory and Resource Management**
- **Supply Chain Operations**
- **Procurement and Logistics Management**
- **Data-Driven Operational Decision Making**

## Analytical Approach

Projects in this section will focus on:

1. Identifying operational challenges and inefficiencies.
2. Analysing operational data and performance indicators.
3. Evaluating processes and resource utilization.
4. Identifying opportunities for process improvement.
5. Developing practical, data-driven recommendations.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- SQL
- Python
- Power BI
- Tableau

*Tools will be incorporated as relevant skills are developed and applied in practical projects.*

## Project Approach

Each project will demonstrate how business data and analytical methods can support operational planning, improve efficiency, optimize processes and enhance business performance.
