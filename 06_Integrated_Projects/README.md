# Integrated Business Analytics Projects

This section showcases end-to-end projects combining business analysis, data analytics and operations management to solve practical business problems.

## Project Categories

- Business Performance and Profitability Analysis
- Sales and Customer Analytics
- Inventory and Demand Analysis
- Supply Chain and Logistics Optimization
- Operational Efficiency and Process Improvement
- Integrated Business Decision-Making

## Project Methodology

1. Define the business problem.
2. Identify business objectives and KPIs.
3. Collect and understand relevant datasets.
4. Clean, transform and analyse data.
5. Identify patterns and operational inefficiencies.
6. Develop dashboards and visualizations.
7. Present actionable business recommendations.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- SQL
- Python
- Microsoft Power BI
- Tableau

## Expected Project Outcomes

- Data-driven business recommendations
- Improved operational efficiency
- Better resource utilization
- Performance and profitability insights
- Evidence-based managerial decisions
