# Business Intelligence

This section focuses on transforming business data into meaningful insights through interactive dashboards, reporting and performance analysis.

## Key Focus Areas

- Business Intelligence and Reporting
- Interactive Dashboard Development
- Key Performance Indicator (KPI) Monitoring
- Business Performance Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Sales and Revenue Analysis
- Operational Performance Reporting
- Management Decision Support

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Tableau
- Microsoft Excel
- SQL

## Analytical Approach

1. Identify business reporting requirements.
2. Prepare and transform relevant datasets.
3. Develop meaningful KPIs and performance metrics.
4. Design interactive dashboards and visualizations.
5. Translate analytical findings into actionable business insights.

## Project Approach

Projects will demonstrate how business intelligence tools can support performance monitoring, identify business trends and improve data-driven managerial decision-making.

*Dashboards and projects will be added as practical skills develop.*
