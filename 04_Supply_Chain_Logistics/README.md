# Supply Chain & Logistics

This section focuses on understanding and analysing supply chain activities to improve operational efficiency, inventory control, procurement and logistics performance.

## Key Focus Areas

- Supply Chain Management
- Procurement and Purchasing Analysis
- Inventory Management
- Demand and Supply Planning
- Vendor Performance Analysis
- Logistics and Distribution Management
- Order Processing and Fulfilment
- Transportation and Cost Analysis

## Analytical Approach

Projects in this section will focus on:

1. Analysing inventory levels and stock movement.
2. Evaluating procurement and supplier performance.
3. Understanding demand patterns and forecasting requirements.
4. Analysing logistics costs and delivery performance.
5. Identifying opportunities to improve supply chain efficiency.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- SQL
- Python
- Power BI
- Tableau

*Tools will be applied as relevant skills are developed.*

## Project Approach

Each project will demonstrate how data analysis can support procurement decisions, inventory optimization, logistics planning, cost control and overall supply chain performance.
